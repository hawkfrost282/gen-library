# Gen Library icon revision

The default is now the simpler ghost-and-books image in `source-app.png`. The cleaner dimensional alternative is `concepts/ghost-library-dimensional.png`. `concepts/dock-comparison.png` compares the previous scene and both revisions at 128, 64 and 32 pixels.

Both revisions were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The original library scene was used only as a character reference. The ghost's large eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny smile and artwork were retained; no letter or G mark was added. The original scene remains in Git history at commit `e45347f`.

The export script trims empty alpha padding, preserves the artwork's proportions, and places it within an 896-pixel safe area on a 1024-pixel transparent canvas. The Dock PNG, general PNG, ICNS and ICO all use that same normalized canvas. Source PNGs are retained without destructive edits.

## Cleaner dimensional version — final prompt

Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Asset type: final macOS app icon for Gen Library. Reference image: use ONLY the friendly ghost's identity from this image—round white ghost, very large dark oval eyes with a simple highlight, rosy cheeks, tiny smile, holding an artwork. Redesign this as a genuinely SIMPLE, readable small Dock icon. One large cream-white ghost fills roughly 65% of a deep midnight-blue rounded square tile; it holds ONE gold-edged art card with an extremely simple navy mountain and a single gold star. A compact row of just THREE broad book spines behind the ghost hints at a library. Bold clean silhouettes, few large shapes, restrained soft 3D/clay shading, polished native-app icon quality. Warm gold and muted teal accents. The face and art card must remain distinct at 32 pixels. No room, no shelves full of tiny books, no architecture, no plants, no lamps, no rug, no background scenery, no detailed picture inside the card. No letters, no G mark, no text or border. Tile centered on a square canvas, with a modest consistent transparent safety margin around the rounded square; outside the rounded tile must be genuinely transparent, never white. Keep all content comfortably within the tile. Return a single finished icon only, not a mockup, not a comparison sheet.

## Simpler version — final prompt

Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Create one much simpler FLAT macOS app icon for Gen Library from this character reference. Preserve the cute friendly white ghost with big black oval eyes, small white eye highlights, rosy cheeks and a tiny smile, holding a small gold-edged picture card. The ghost must be the dominant easy-to-recognize silhouette. Reduce the books behind it to just TWO simple broad rounded book-spine blocks in muted teal and warm gold, with only one short line each. The picture card contains just one simple two-peak mountain shape and one small gold star. Flat clean illustration with smooth solid color shapes and tiny amounts of restrained soft shading; absolutely no material texture, no shiny glass eyes, no ornamental book patterns, no complex gradients, no bloom, no photorealism, no room scene. Dark midnight-blue rounded-square tile with genuinely transparent outside corners and a modest safety margin. Bold readable design at 32px. Face large and unobstructed, picture card secondary, books tertiary. No text, no letters, no G, no watermark, no mockup, no comparison grid. Return one single finished app icon.
